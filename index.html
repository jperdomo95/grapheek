How we teach

A simple method that creates real progress

  1. 1. Placement & goals

    We start with your current level and define goals that fit your routine and motivation.

  2. 2. Guided practice

    Every class combines clear explanations with immediate speaking and listening practice as well as interactive games to make sure you fully understand each lesson.

  3. 3. Feedback & growth

    You receive tailored feedback and corrections after each class. We detect your pain points and work on them.
